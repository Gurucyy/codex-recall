# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want `codex-recall` to be a useful, respectful, and privacy-conscious
open source project. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to participate in
a way that keeps discussions technical, constructive, and safe for people who may be
working with sensitive local data.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while still being precise about technical risks.
- Keep feedback focused on code, documentation, safety, privacy, and project direction.
- Use synthetic examples when discussing bugs, diagnostics, and repair flows.
- Avoid posting private transcripts, local paths, tokens, backups, reports, or exports.
- Accept maintainer decisions when they are made to preserve the project's safety model.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information or local data.
- Pressuring contributors to share sensitive Codex session evidence publicly.
- Encouraging unsafe repair behavior, destructive data handling, or secret disclosure.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after maintainers have clarified project boundaries.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
and community spaces connected to this project.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through a private channel
when one is available. If no private channel is listed yet, open a minimal public issue
asking maintainers to provide a private contact path. Do not include private transcripts,
tokens, local paths, or sensitive personal details in public reports.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open source conduct policies, including the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), and adapted for a
privacy-sensitive local recovery tool.
